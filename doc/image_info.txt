The [L] asks for a landscape image. Once you have the prompt working well, you can try [L2] which makes a bigger image with higher quality. There are other options for different aspect ratios:

[V] very tall
[v]
[T] tall
[t]
[P] portrait (the default)
[p]
[S] square
[l]
[L] landscape
[w]
[W] wide
[x]
[X] extra wide

the lower case options are in between. The main ones I use are [P], [S], [L] but [W] is also good for monitor wallpaper, and [T] or maybe [v] or [V] for phone wallpaper.

You can add a 2 to any of them for higher quality, like [L2] [P2] [S2] etc. There are other quality options, but none (0) or 2 are the best ones to use.
